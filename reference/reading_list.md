# Reading list

**Core (owning these two covers most of the curriculum)**
- JSBSim Reference Manual (RM): PDF <https://jsbsim.sourceforge.net/JSBSimReferenceManual.pdf>,
  web edition <https://jsbsim-team.github.io/jsbsim-reference-manual/>
- R. Beard, T. McLain, *Small Unmanned Aircraft: Theory and Practice* (Princeton, 2012), with
  the companion code <https://github.com/randybeard/mavsim_public>

**On your desk at work**
- B. Stevens, F. Lewis, E. Johnson, *Aircraft Control and Simulation*, 3rd ed. (Wiley, 2016):
  modeling, linearization, robustness, the reference for "how do real programs do this?"
- V. Klein, E. Morelli, *Aircraft System Identification*, 2nd ed. (2016): everything in Module 14

**Refreshers**
- R. Nelson, *Flight Stability and Automatic Control*, 2nd ed.: modes and stability derivatives
- G. Franklin, J. D. Powell, A. Emami-Naeini, *Feedback Control of Dynamic Systems*: loop shaping, margins
- R. Labbe, *Kalman and Bayesian Filters in Python* (free notebooks): the KF/EKF intuition
- D. Simon, *Optimal State Estimation*: the KF/EKF mathematics

**V&V and flight test**
- NASA-STD-7009A, *Standard for Models and Simulations*: credibility of simulations
- J. Hanson, B. Beard, "Applying Monte Carlo Simulation to Launch Vehicle Design and
  Requirements Verification", *J. Spacecraft and Rockets*, 2012: pass-rate statistics
- R. Kimberlin, *Flight Testing of Fixed-Wing Aircraft* (AIAA): test cards, data reduction
- MIL-F-8785C (flying qualities, turbulence model) and MIL-HDBK-1797

**Software**
- JSBSim source and API docs: <https://github.com/JSBSim-Team/jsbsim>, <https://jsbsim-team.github.io/jsbsim/>
- python-control: <https://python-control.readthedocs.io>
- *Pro Git*, ch. 3 (branching, rebasing): <https://git-scm.com/book>
